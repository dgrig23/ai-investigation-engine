Incident report INC-2029: Email notification delays

Summary
Email notification delays (SEV-3). On 2026-01-22 the incident started at 09:10 UTC and was resolved at 12:10 UTC, a duration of 180 minutes. Impact: password-reset and deposit-receipt emails were delayed by up to three hours.

Affected services
notification-service (owner: Platform team).

Root cause
The root cause was the email vendor applied a sending rate limit after a marketing campaign burst.

Follow-up
Action items are tracked in the reliability review.
